Turn your family tree into a poster

Make a beautiful, print-ready family tree from a GEDCOM file in three steps. Free, and nothing leaves your computer.

  1. 1Add your family fileDrop a GEDCOM (.ged) here, or choose one on the left. Most family tree websites and programs can export one.
  2. 2Pick a styleA tree with branches and roots, or a classic chart. Choose colours, a title and a border, and add photos if you like.
  3. 3Download your posterSave it as a sharp PNG, PDF or SVG, sized from A4 up to A0, ready for any printer.
Where do I get a GEDCOM file?

Look for Export or Download GEDCOM in your family tree website or software. Only the owner of a tree can usually export it.
